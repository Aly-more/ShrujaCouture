Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about orders, shipping, payments and support at Shruja Couture.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 business days. Delivery timelines may vary depending on your location.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Currently, we do not offer returns or exchanges. Please review product details carefully before placing your order.

How can I contact Shruja Couture?

You can connect with us through our Contact Us page, Instagram or WhatsApp for any assistance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards and all Razorpay supported payment methods.

How do I track my order?

A dedicated tracking feature is currently under development. Until then, please contact our support team for order updates.

Is shipping free?

Yes, we offer free shipping on orders above Rs.1499 across India.

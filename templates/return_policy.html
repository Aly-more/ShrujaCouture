RETURN POLICY

No Return & Exchange Policy

Every Shruja Couture piece is carefully inspected before dispatch to ensure it meets our quality standards. As a result, all purchases are considered final.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Final Sale Policy

At Shruja Couture, we currently do not offer returns, exchanges, or refunds once an order has been placed and successfully delivered.

2. Why We Follow This Policy

  • Every product is individually inspected before dispatch.
  • We maintain strict quality checks for every order.
  • Product descriptions and sizing information are provided to help customers make informed purchases.

3. Before You Place Your Order

  • Review the product description carefully.
  • Check the size guide before ordering.
  • Verify your shipping address and contact details.

4. Exceptional Situations

If you receive an incorrect product or your order arrives damaged due to shipping, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photographs. Our team will review the issue and assist you accordingly.

5. Need Assistance?

If you have any questions before placing your order, we're always happy to help you choose the right product and size.

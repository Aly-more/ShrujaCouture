SHIPPING POLICY

Reliable Delivery, Thoughtfully Packed

Every Shruja Couture order is carefully packed and shipped with attention to detail, ensuring your purchase reaches you safely and beautifully.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

2. Estimated Delivery

  • Metro Cities: 2–5 business days.
  • Other Cities: 4–8 business days.
  • Remote locations may require additional time.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, are calculated and displayed during checkout. Any promotional free shipping offers will also be shown before payment.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you'll receive tracking details via email or SMS (where available), allowing you to follow your shipment until it arrives.

5. Delivery Delays

  • Weather conditions.
  • Public holidays.
  • Festival seasons.
  • Courier partner delays.

6. Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address, contact number, and PIN code are accurate before placing your order. Shruja Couture cannot be held responsible for delays caused by incorrect shipping details.

7. Need Assistance?

If you have any questions regarding your shipment, feel free to contact our support team. We'll be happy to assist you.

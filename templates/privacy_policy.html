PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring a safe shopping experience at Shruja Couture.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you browse or shop with Shruja Couture, we may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, billing address, and order details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Improve our website and shopping experience.
  • Notify you about new collections and offers (only if you choose to receive them).

3. Payment Security

Payments are securely processed through trusted payment gateways. Shruja Couture does not store your debit card, credit card, or banking information.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze website traffic.

5. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal information. Your details are shared only with trusted delivery, payment, and technology partners when necessary to complete your order.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.

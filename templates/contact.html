Frequently Asked Questions

Every order is carefully prepared before dispatch. Orders are usually shipped within 1–2 business days, with delivery taking approximately 3–7 business days depending on your location. You'll receive tracking details as soon as your order is on its way.

At Shruja Couture, all sales are final and we do not offer returns or exchanges. We encourage you to review the product description and size guide carefully before placing your order. If you need help selecting the right size, feel free to contact us before purchasing.

Every product includes a detailed size guide to help you find the perfect fit. If you're still unsure, simply reach out to us—we'll be happy to assist you before you place your order.

Once your order has been dispatched, we'll send you a confirmation email and/or WhatsApp message with your tracking details so you can follow your order every step of the way.
